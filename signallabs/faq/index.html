TideLine Strategies

Questions about TideLine, answered.

A straightforward guide to our services, how projects start, and what you can do with Loki today.

What does TideLine Strategies do?

TideLine provides business operations consulting, workflow automation, and custom software for owner-led service businesses. We begin with how the business runs, identify where work gets stuck, and choose a useful first change.

What kinds of businesses does TideLine work with?

Our strongest operating context is gymnastics, cheer, dance, wellness, and appointment-heavy service teams. We also scope work for other owner-led service businesses where recurring admin work and unclear handoffs consume staff time.

Where should we start if we do not know what needs fixing?

Start with one task that repeatedly pulls you or your staff away from other work. An operational diagnostic looks at the workflow, tools, records, and handoffs before choosing a software solution.

Do I need new software before an operational diagnostic?

No. TideLine starts with the people, workflow, and tools you already use. A clearer owner, a simpler handoff, or a more dependable record may be the first useful change.

Does every workflow automation need AI?

No. A predictable rule or an existing software feature may be enough. AI is one option for tasks such as drafting or interpreting text, with review and exception handling matched to the risk.

Can TideLine work with our current tools?

Working with the systems you already use is the starting point. Integration options depend on the provider, subscription, data access, and permissions. We confirm those details before building a plan around a feature.

How much does a custom software project cost?

TideLine scopes custom work around the workflow, integrations, permissions, and ongoing support it needs. Share the problem and existing tools first so the team can discuss scope and pricing; there is no published one-size-fits-all build price.

Who owns the accounts, data, and documentation?

You own the accounts, data, and documentation for your custom build. Hosting, maintenance, access, and support responsibilities should be agreed as part of the project scope.

What is Ask Loki?

Ask Loki is TideLine’s complimentary interactive business audit. Describe your business and work through a problem in a conversation. You can question the advice, correct its assumptions, and ask for a revised plan. It does not need private customer records or passwords.

Does Ask Loki remember my conversation?

An anonymous refresh starts a new conversation. If you choose to save with your email, TideLine adds your details to its private CRM and saves the conversation and customer-specific Loki memory for later restoration in that browser. Do not enter secrets or private customer records.

Is the Loki app available now?

The Loki app is coming soon. Join the waitlist with your mobile number to receive a launch text. No messages are sent at signup. Ask Loki on the TideLine website is available separately.

Does Loki send messages or change my business systems?

The public Ask Loki audit works from what you share in the conversation. It does not connect to your inbox or business accounts and does not send customer messages or make changes in those systems.

How do I contact TideLine?

Use the contact form on the TideLine website or email Cody Knudsen at c.knudsen@tidelinestrats.com. Describe the recurring work, who handles it, and the tools you currently use.
