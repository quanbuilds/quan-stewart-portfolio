Our story

TideLine Strategies is an AI consulting and custom app development company for small and mid-sized B2B businesses — we find the operational friction eating away precious employee time, then build systems that align with where your company is headed and accelerate that growth with scale in mind.

Cody and Quan have known each other since middle school, reconnecting years later back home in Illinois after paths through California and the Southeast. A few years into a sales career, Cody was ready for a new problem to solve; months of trading ideas with Quan about AI, cybersecurity, and SaaS led to one: federal contracts are all public on SAM.gov, but sourcing and bidding on them was still a slow, manual grind. Cody had the idea, Quan built it — and the work showed them the same friction shows up everywhere, just with different paperwork. That's the thread TideLine has followed ever since.

Meet Cody Knudsen

Cody Knudsen started his career in door-to-door technology sales, knocking on doors, cold calling, and learning early how to earn trust face-to-face. That experience became the foundation for everything that followed.

He went on to sell managed IT services and managed security services, bringing IT, cybersecurity, software, and hardware solutions to small and mid-sized businesses across every industry. He worked for an organization with a strong culture and a sales-first mindset, and there he moved from selling products to consulting. He learned to understand how a business runs before recommending the tools to support it.

Cody co-founded Tideline Strategies with one focused goal: automating the federal contracting process. The application worked, and building it gave the team deep experience with government regulations, compliance, and the complex processes behind federal procurement. That experience shaped what Tideline Strategies is today.

Tideline Strategies is now a business and technology consultancy that helps business owners and professionals improve their operations and turn their ideas into working software. Every engagement starts with the same approach: understand the people, process, and culture first, and then build solutions that fit where the business is today and where it's going.

Cody Knudsen / Founder & CEO

Meet Quan Stewart

A technical builder who understands service work.

I built computers as a kid, started designing webpages around 11, and spent years teaching myself complex creative and technical systems. Coaching gymnastics taught me the part that matters most here: find the source of the problem, explain it clearly, and apply the right correction.

My path has not been polished or traditional. I did not finish college and I do not have a computer science degree. I have worked manual labor, coached preschoolers through regional champions, served as senior gym staff, and spent the last several years obsessively studying AI, automation, entrepreneurship, and how modern tools can change the economics of a small business.

I built systems across operations, customer intake, reporting, communications, and agent workflows. TideLine Strategies is where those lessons become one focused commercial service for owner-led businesses. I am starting where I have the deepest operating context — gymnastics, cheer, dance, wellness, and appointment-heavy service teams — while building methods that apply anywhere repeated computer work is consuming payroll.

Quan Stewart / Founder and systems builder
