TideLine Strategies

Operations first. Useful software where it fits.

TideLine Strategies helps owner-led service businesses find operational friction, strengthen the workflow, and build practical systems with the people who use them.

Choose the starting point that fits the problem.

Business operations consulting

TideLine helps service business owners understand where work gets stuck, make responsibility clear, and choose a practical first change. We start with intake, scheduling, follow-up, reporting, and the people doing the work.

Explore business operations consulting ↗

Workflow automation

TideLine helps owner-led service businesses reduce repeated computer work after the underlying process is clear. We look for dependable, bounded tasks that can be automated without moving important decisions away from your team.

Explore workflow automation ↗

Custom software development

TideLine builds practical tools for owner-led service businesses when existing software leaves an important workflow gap. We define the work with the people doing it, then scope a build around the problem the business needs to solve.

Explore custom software development ↗

How we work together

  1. Find the friction. Map the task, the records, and the handoffs with the people doing the work.
  2. Fix the foundation. Clarify ownership and make the existing source of truth dependable.
  3. Build where it helps. Test a useful change, then add automation or custom software where the workflow calls for it.

Who we work with

Our strongest starting lane is gymnastics, cheer, dance, wellness, and appointment-heavy service teams. The same approach applies to other owner-led service businesses where repeated computer work is consuming staff time.
